HOW TO USE

How YouAreNext works

One queue, two sides. Pick yours — mostly pictures, very little reading.

Join a queue

🔍Find your placeSearch salons, clinics and cafés near you.
→
🎫Join the queueTap Join queue and pick your service.
→
👀Track your turnSee your number and the live wait time.
→
🔔It's your turnWalk in when you're called. No standing in line.

At the shop

📷Scan or tapScan the QR at the shop, or join from home.
→
☑️Pick your serviceChoose one or more services.
→
🎫Get your spotSee your number and the live wait time.
⭐Rate your visitAfter a visit, open Rate & review and tap the stars.
🚪Leave anytimeTap Leave queue if your plans change.
♥Save favoritesTap the heart on a place to find it faster.

Set up once

📝Register your businessSign up as a provider with your services.
→
✅Get approvedWe check and approve your listing.
→
🕘Add hours & servicesSet opening hours and time per service.
→
🟢Open your queueTap Open for walk-ins on your dashboard.

Run your day

📱Customers joinFrom the app, your QR code, or walk-ins.
→
👆Start nextOne tap calls the next customer.
→
🛎️ServeServe one or many at the same time.
→
✅CompleteFinish to free the next spot.

Configure in Settings

🕘Opening hoursSettings → Your day: open, close and buffer time.
👥Staff servingSettings: how many customers you serve at once.
⏱️Time per serviceSettings: minutes per service, for better waits.
📷Your QR codeQueue tab: print it for your counter.
🚶Walk-insQueue tab: add in-person customers yourself.
🔒Private listingSettings: hide from search, join by QR only.
📊Reports & reviewsReports tab: your day in numbers and stars.
