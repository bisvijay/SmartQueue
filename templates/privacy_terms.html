Privacy & terms

How NextInQueue handles your information, and the rules that apply when you use the service. NextInQueue is operated from India and this page is written for our users in India.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and phone number when you register.
  • Queue bookings: the businesses you queue at, the services you pick, and your position and wait history.
  • Walk-in details: if you join a queue at a shop counter or by scanning a QR code, the name and contact details you enter are shared with that business so it can call your turn.
  • Business details: for providers — business name, address, services, timings, and contact information shown on the public listing.
  • Preferences: your language and theme choices are stored on your own device (in your browser), not on our servers.

Why we use it

We use this information only to run the queues: to save your place, show live wait times, let businesses manage their lines, and keep accounts secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

A business can see the name, contact details, and booked services of customers in its own queue — nothing more. We share data with the hosting and infrastructure providers that keep NextInQueue running, only as needed to operate the service, and when required by law.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account is active. Completed queue visits may be kept for a limited period for the business's records and to improve wait estimates, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, connections to NextInQueue are encrypted, and access to personal information is limited to what the service needs to function. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care with your data.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent for its use. Write to us at info@nextinqueue.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

NextInQueue is meant for users aged 18 and above. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will update this page and note the new date above.

Terms of Service

The service

NextInQueue provides virtual queues for local businesses. Customers join a queue, see an estimated wait, and are called when it is their turn. Wait times are estimates based on the services booked and each business's typical service times — they are not a guarantee, and a business may occasionally run ahead of or behind the estimate.

Accounts

You are responsible for the accuracy of the details you register with and for activity under your account. Keep your password private. One person or business should not operate multiple accounts to gain an unfair place in a queue.

Acceptable use

  • Join a queue only when you genuinely intend to use the service.
  • Do not hold places for resale, block queues with fake bookings, or misuse another person's contact details.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, scrape, or reverse-engineer the service.

For businesses

Providers are responsible for keeping their listing, services, and timings accurate, for managing their queue fairly and in order, and for using customer details shared through NextInQueue only to serve those customers.

Availability

We work to keep NextInQueue available at all times, but the service may be interrupted for maintenance or reasons beyond our control. We are not liable for losses arising from a delayed or missed turn, though we will always try to make things right — write to us.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute will first be addressed through good-faith discussion with us at info@nextinqueue.in.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time; the current version will always be on this page. Continuing to use NextInQueue after a change means you accept the updated terms.

Questions?

For anything about this page, write to info@nextinqueue.in or see our contact page.
